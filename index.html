Setup Guide
  1. Create a new Google Sheet (give it any name).
  2. In the menu: Extensions → Apps Script.
  3. Delete what's there, paste the script below, and Save.
  4. Click Deploy → New deployment. Type: Web app. Execute as Me. Who has access: Anyone. Click Deploy and authorize.
  5. Copy the Web app URL it gives you and paste it above, then tap Connect & Test.
The iPad must be online for live sync. Offline sign-ins are kept safe and sent next time you tap Sync All Pending.
